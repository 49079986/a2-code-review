04

前置知识与数据库背景

一个连接是一个独立服务器会话，用pg_backend_pid辨认。BEGIN到COMMIT/ROLLBACK是一笔事务；未提交写入通常不对其他会话可见。默认READ COMMITTED每条命令取得新快照。行锁协调冲突写入；快照回答可见性，不能代替锁。

把解释落实到代码步骤

时间线：T1 BEGIN→查到Return；T2 BEGIN→同车Return→写未来Reserve→COMMIT；T1 INSERT Collect→触发器看到已提交Reserve→异常→ROLLBACK。第一次检查结果已过时。

READ COMMITTED通常每条命令取新快照，事务打开不等于固定起始快照。触发器在发起操作的事务内部执行，没有神奇的全局最新特权；此处命令发生于T2提交之后，足以看到Reserve。UDF中的BEGIN是PL/pgSQL代码块，不会再开独立SQL事务。

业务结果是停车站会员被拒绝，因为另一会员三天后预约已成为最新状态。协调检查与写入能减少竞争窗口；若要合理支持未来预约，还需记录起止时间并校验区间冲突。仅加锁不能修复“未来等于现在”的模型。

日期时间函数

NOW · 事务起点时间

NOW() → timestamp with time zone

无参数，返回当前事务开始时间，事务内多次调用保持稳定。

本作业中的作用
Q4记录现在的Collect，以及NOW()+INTERVAL '3 days'的Reserve。
输入与输出示例
教学：同一事务等10秒再NOW()仍是事务开始时间；加3 days是三天后的时间值。
边界与常见错误
event_time为timestamp without time zone，赋值时按会话时区转换，不能忽略环境。想读墙钟是另一函数clock_timestamp，原代码未使用。

READ COMMITTED / snapshot · 读已提交快照

SHOW transaction_isolation;

默认READ COMMITTED每条命令看到该命令快照允许的已提交内容和自身可见修改。

本作业中的作用
T1先见Return，T2提交后T1后续Collect触发器见Reserve。
输入与输出示例
教学：两条SELECT之间别人提交更新，第二条可以读到不同值。
边界与常见错误

触发器与PL/pgSQL

BI_COLLECT / NEW / RAISE EXCEPTION

BEFORE INSERT ... FOR EACH ROW EXECUTE FUNCTION UDF_BI_COLLECT();

每个插入候选行之前调用函数；NEW是拟插入记录；RETURN NEW允许继续，RAISE EXCEPTION拒绝并抛错。

本作业中的作用
Collect时读该车按时间/ID最新事件，检查Incident、Collect或其他会员Reserve。
输入与输出示例
教学：last_type=Reserve且last_member=2、NEW.member_id=1时抛Vehicle ... reserved。
边界与常见错误
Reserve本身不进入Collect检查；最新未来事件也会当当前状态。NEW还不是已提交表行；触发器没有独立事务。

The schedule is T1 BEGIN and availability read; T2 BEGIN and availability read; T2 
Reserve and COMMIT; T1 Collect attempt; trigger exception; T1 ROLLBACK. T1 initially 
sees an available car, but that answer becomes stale before its next operation. 
At READ COMMITTED, each statement uses a fresh snapshot. The Collect attempt runs 
after T2 has committed, so the trigger can see the committed Reserve. The trigger 
orders events by event time and treats that future-dated reservation as the latest state. 
Because it belongs to member 2, it refuses a Collect by member 1. 
An ordinary SELECT reads without a row lock that reserves this car. Its ACCESS SHARE 
relation lock is compatible with the other transaction's insertion, so it does not prevent 
T2 from changing the answer. T1 stays open but does not retain its first statement's 
snapshot for later statements. In this schedule the rejection is a business-rule 
exception, with no circular lock wait. 
For the co-operative, the person at the parking bay is refused the car even though the 
other person reserved it three days ahead. The simplified trigger makes a future 
reservation an immediate hold. Coordinating availability checks and writes would 
address races, while interval-aware booking rules would also be needed to distinguish 
available now from reserved later.
